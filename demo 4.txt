# Mission Workflow Validation

## Startup

  cd ~/Documents/Polaris/PX4-Autopilot
  ./scripts/start_polaris_sim.sh

  cd ~/Documents/Polaris-ground
  VITE_VEHICLE_PROVIDER=mavlink pnpm tauri dev

Confirm Connected, SYS-1, live telemetry, and a live Operator Map.

## Draft and verification

1. Create local draft waypoints with map clicks and Mission controls.
2. Upload using the Mission confirmation dialog. Upload acceptance alone is not readback verification.
3. Download from vehicle. Vehicle Mission appears only from the readback.
4. Confirm Local Draft and Vehicle Mission report In Sync when their supported fields match.
5. Edit/reorder a draft item. Confirm Draft Differs and Vehicle Mission stays read-only.
6. Download again. Confirm the modified draft is not overwritten.
7. Choose Use Vehicle Mission as Draft, confirm adoption, and verify In Sync.
8. Clear vehicle mission with confirmation. Confirm Local Draft remains and Vehicle Mission is empty.
9. Re-upload the retained draft, read back, and confirm In Sync.

## Map regression

Verify map click-to-add, map/list selection, coordinate edit, reorder, remove, Use Current Vehicle Position, Fit Mission, draft polyline, and vehicle-readback polyline.

## Transfer loss

Only while safely disarmed, begin a mission download then run:

  cd ~/Documents/Polaris/PX4-Autopilot
  ./scripts/stop_polaris_sim.sh

Confirm the transfer fails and does not resume. Restore PX4 with:

  ./scripts/start_polaris_sim.sh

Confirm a new explicit download succeeds.

## Safe shutdown

Land/disarm first. Close Ground, then run:

  cd ~/Documents/Polaris/PX4-Autopilot
  ./scripts/stop_polaris_sim.sh

Do not use pkill. Identify any remaining process with ps/lsof and use only `kill -TERM <exact-pid>`.

## Bug checklist

Record step, connection state, draft sync state, vehicle mission state, transfer result, map behavior, PX4/Gazebo behavior, and exact error text.
