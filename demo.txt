==================================================
POLARIS GROUND DEMO RUNBOOK
==================================================

USE ONLY: /Applications/Polaris Ground.app
DO NOT RUN: pnpm tauri dev, Vite, QGroundControl, or another MAVSDK/Polaris flight app.

Packaged connection: Ground 127.0.0.1:14540 <-> PX4 127.0.0.1:14580

==================================================
1. PRE-DEMO SETUP
==================================================

* Close QGroundControl and all MAVSDK/Polaris flight apps.
* Connect power; silence notifications.
* Open one Terminal for PX4 and keep it open.
* Arrange Gazebo beside Polaris Ground before Manual Flight.
* During Manual Flight, keep Ground focused. Clicking/hiding another app disables it.

Optional clean start:

cd ~/Documents/Polaris/PX4-Autopilot
./scripts/stop_polaris_sim.sh

==================================================
2. START PX4 / GAZEBO
==================================================

Terminal:

cd ~/Documents/Polaris/PX4-Autopilot
POLARIS_WORLD=antarctica ./scripts/start_polaris_sim.sh

The launcher configures the environment and automatically starts the Gazebo server,
separate macOS Gazebo GUI, gz_x500_mono_cam vehicle, and PX4 SITL.

WAIT FOR:
* Gazebo opens with the stationary X500.
* Terminal prints "Gazebo world antarctica is ready".
* PX4 completes startup and keeps running.

[SHOW GAZEBO] [PAUSE 2 SEC]

==================================================
3. OPEN POLARIS GROUND
==================================================

Second Terminal tab/window:

open "/Applications/Polaris Ground.app"

Allow local-network access if requested.

WAIT FOR:
* Operate workspace renders.
* Top bar shows CONNECTED.
* Vehicle identity shows Vehicle 1 and SYS-1.

[SHOW POLARIS GROUND] [PAUSE 2 SEC]

==================================================
4. VERIFY CONNECTION
==================================================

Confirm on Operate:
* CONNECTED and SYS-1 are visible.
* Vehicle summary and timeline update.
* Vehicle action buttons are enabled.
* Manual Flight says DISABLED.

If SYS-1 is absent, use DEMO RECOVERY. Do not issue commands.

==================================================
5. SHOW TELEMETRY
==================================================

[SHOW POLARIS GROUND]

Show: battery, altitude, ground speed, link quality, heading, mission summary,
and Recent activity.

[PAUSE 2 SEC FOR TELEMETRY]

==================================================
6. ARM
==================================================

1. Click Arm.
2. Click Confirm Arm in "Arm?".
3. Wait for "Arm accepted".
4. Confirm "Vehicle armed".

Continue to Take off within about 5 seconds; PX4 may auto-disarm if left on the ground.

==================================================
7. TAKEOFF
==================================================

1. Click Take off.
2. Click Confirm Take off in "Take off?".
3. Wait for "Take off accepted".
4. Watch altitude rise; wait for a stable airborne hover.

[SHOW GAZEBO DURING CLIMB]
[RETURN FOCUS TO POLARIS GROUND]

==================================================
8. ENABLE MANUAL FLIGHT
==================================================

1. Keep Polaris Ground focused.
2. Click Enable Keyboard Control.
3. Do not press movement keys yet.

WAIT FOR: PREPARING -> ENTERING OFFBOARD -> Offboard -> ENABLED NEUTRAL.
Confirm Input says Neutral setpoint.

[WAIT FOR ENABLED NEUTRAL]

Do not continue unless Offboard and ENABLED NEUTRAL are visible.

==================================================
9. WASD MOVEMENT
==================================================

Keep Ground focused. After every release, wait for Neutral setpoint.

* [HOLD W ~1.5 SEC] Forward; expect ACTIVE and about 0.5 m/s. [RELEASE W]
* [HOLD S ~1 SEC] Backward. [RELEASE S]
* [HOLD A ~1 SEC] Left. [RELEASE A]
* [HOLD D ~1 SEC] Right. [RELEASE D]

[OBSERVE GAZEBO WITHOUT CLICKING IT]

==================================================
10. ALTITUDE CONTROL
==================================================

Only continue with several metres of ground clearance.

* [HOLD ARROW UP ~1 SEC] Climb slightly. [RELEASE ARROW UP]
* Wait for Neutral setpoint and note altitude increase.
* [HOLD ARROW DOWN ~1 SEC] Return near prior altitude. [RELEASE ARROW DOWN]
* Wait for Neutral setpoint.

WARNING: Never hold Arrow Down near the ground.

==================================================
11. YAW CONTROL
==================================================

* Note heading.
* [HOLD ARROW LEFT ~1 SEC] Yaw left. [RELEASE ARROW LEFT]
* Confirm heading changes; wait for Neutral setpoint.
* [HOLD ARROW RIGHT ~1 SEC] Yaw right. [RELEASE ARROW RIGHT]
* Confirm heading changes back; wait for Neutral setpoint.

==================================================
12. COMBINED MOVEMENT
==================================================

1. [HOLD W + D TOGETHER ~1 SEC]
2. Confirm both W and D indicators are active.
3. [RELEASE W AND D]
4. Wait for ENABLED NEUTRAL / Neutral setpoint.

Do not hold combined movement longer than 1 second.

==================================================
13. SAFETY / ESCAPE
==================================================

1. From ENABLED NEUTRAL, press Escape once.
2. Confirm Manual Flight says DISABLED and Input says Neutral setpoint.
3. Wait for ground speed to return to 0.0 m/s.
4. Confirm the vehicle remains armed. Escape must NOT disarm.

[PAUSE 2 SEC ON DISABLED + 0.0 m/s]

SAFETY:
* Manual Flight must be DISABLED before Mission, RTL, or Land.
* Never trigger RTL/Land while Manual Flight owns Offboard.
* Unexpected movement: release all keys, press Escape, then Land.

==================================================
14. MISSION WORKSPACE
==================================================

This transfers one waypoint; it does NOT execute the mission.

1. Confirm Manual Flight is DISABLED; click Mission.
2. Show live Vehicle Position.
3. Keep "Untitled mission" or enter "Demo waypoint".
4. Click Add item once; leave type as Waypoint.
5. For item 1, click Use position.
6. Enter 10 in Altitude m; leave Hold s and Radius m at 0.
7. Wait for "Mission validation passed."

Upload:
1. Click Upload mission -> Confirm Upload mission.
2. Wait for "Latest transfer accepted" / "Mission upload accepted."

Readback:
1. Click Download from vehicle -> Confirm Download mission.
2. Wait for "Mission download completed."
3. Show item 1 under Confirmed vehicle plan.

Cleanup:
1. Click Clear vehicle mission -> Confirm Clear mission.
2. Wait for "Mission cleared."

[PAUSE 2 SEC ON TRANSFER RESULT]

==================================================
15. RETURN TO LAUNCH
==================================================

1. Click Operate.
2. Confirm Manual Flight is DISABLED and vehicle is armed/airborne.
3. Click Return to launch.
4. Click Confirm Return to launch in "Return to launch?".
5. Wait for "Return to launch accepted" and mode Return.

[SHOW GAZEBO] [PAUSE 3-5 SEC]

Do not send Land until RTL is accepted. PX4 may land automatically.

==================================================
16. LAND
==================================================

If RTL already landed and disarmed: do not send Land again.

If still airborne after Return is established:
1. Focus Ground; confirm Manual Flight is DISABLED.
2. Click Land -> Confirm Land.
3. Wait for "Land accepted", descending altitude, then "Vehicle disarmed".

Never click Disarm while airborne.

==================================================
17. FINAL STATE
==================================================

Confirm:
* Vehicle disarmed. If landed but still armed, click Disarm -> Confirm Disarm.
* Altitude is near ground level; speed is 0.0 m/s.
* Manual Flight is DISABLED.
* Latest command is accepted; Ground remains CONNECTED.

[SHOW POLARIS GROUND FINAL STATE]
[SHOW GAZEBO VEHICLE ON GROUND] [PAUSE 3 SEC]

==================================================
18. SHUTDOWN
==================================================

1. Confirm landed and disarmed.
2. Quit Polaris Ground with Command-Q.
3. In the PX4 Terminal press Ctrl+C; wait for cleanup.

If that Terminal was closed or cleanup failed:

cd ~/Documents/Polaris/PX4-Autopilot
./scripts/stop_polaris_sim.sh

Never use pkill, killall, or broad process matching.

==================================================
DEMO RECOVERY
==================================================

GROUND DOES NOT CONNECT
* Quit Ground; close QGroundControl/MAVSDK/other flight apps using UDP 14540.
* Run the canonical stop script; restart PX4/Gazebo; reopen the installed app.
* Optional check: lsof -nP -iUDP:14540 -iUDP:14580

PX4 DOES NOT ARM
* Do not spam Arm. Read the result and PX4 Terminal status.
* Wait for CONNECTED, SYS-1, valid position, and stable telemetry; retry once.

OFFBOARD FAILS / MANUAL FLIGHT STAYS TRANSITIONING
* Press Escape; if available click Disable Keyboard Control.
* Wait for DISABLED / Neutral setpoint. Retry once only after stable telemetry.

UNEXPECTED MOVEMENT
* Release all keys; press Escape; confirm DISABLED and 0.0 m/s.
* Click Land once, confirm, and wait for disarm.

UNSTABLE SIMULATOR
* If Ground responds: Escape, Land, wait for disarm, then quit Ground.
* Run only:

  cd ~/Documents/Polaris/PX4-Autopilot
  ./scripts/stop_polaris_sim.sh

* Restart from PRE-DEMO SETUP.
